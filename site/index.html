Why Gantrics
Stephen Poppe · Founder

Built by someone who has been the buyer.

I'm Stephen Poppe. I've been a project manager at a top-10 GC. I've run R&D at a national contractor. I've led sales teams selling into the biggest general contractors in the country, and I've built customer success functions for construction technology startups from scratch. I've deployed technology on jobsites and watched it fail. I've watched it succeed, too. Less often than vendors would like to admit.

Gantrics started as a consultancy that told contech companies the truth about their messaging. The shows started as a way to stay close to the industry. Somewhere along the way the shows became the point.

Every week we sit down with the builders, operators, and decision-makers contech companies are trying to reach. That is a living connection to how the industry actually thinks, talks, and decides. Sponsors get access to it. The audience gets stories worth their time. That is the whole business.
