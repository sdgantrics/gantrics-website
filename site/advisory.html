The problem

Most construction tech marketing is written for investors, not buyers.

You lead with features. You lean on words like "streamline" and "revolutionize." Your case studies read like press releases. And the PMs, supers, and VPs you are trying to reach scroll right past it. Not because your product is bad. Because your story doesn't sound like someone who has ever stood in a trailer.

What they wrote
"Streamline your project workflows with our AI-powered platform built for modern construction teams."
What a PM actually thinks
Cool. What does it do?

The gap isn't talent. Your marketing team is probably good. The gap is perspective. You need someone who has sat through the demos, managed the rollouts, and watched what actually gets adopted. Someone who can tell you what a VP of Preconstruction thinks when they read your homepage.

Story Partner: the advisory and the story, in one engagement.

Story Partner pairs our advisory work with Construction Conversations. You get everything in Featured, an Insider Take article in Construction Briefs, and the advisory below. We quote it per client, after a first conversation about what your team actually needs.

What's included

  • Everything in Featured: a guest episode a quarter, your name on every episode, and a host read in every episode
  • An Insider Take article in Construction Briefs, in the voice of one of your customer champions or someone on your team
  • Two 60-minute working sessions at the start on ICP, positioning and go-to-market, then one a month
  • A messaging review twice a year: your homepage, your deck and one sales email
  • A monthly field intel note, with a section written for your team that only your team sees
  • A one-page "say it this way" sheet after each story
  • Prep briefs before the conferences we're both attending

Start with Spotlight, and that month counts as month one of Featured.

How the two halves fit: The episodes and the Insider Take put your customers' story in front of the field. The sessions and reviews make sure your homepage, your deck and your sales team tell the same story once the episode is out.

Stephen keeps editorial control of every episode and every piece we make together. You review for facts before anything goes out, and if we got one wrong, we fix it. That's written into the terms.
