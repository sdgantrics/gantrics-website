Advisory

Your story doesn't land the way you think it does.

Gantrics is a media company first. But the reason the shows work is the same reason a handful of contech companies still ask us to look at their go-to-market: we have been the buyer, and we talk to your buyers every week. We take on a small number of advisory engagements each year.

The problem

Most construction tech marketing is written for investors, not buyers.

You lead with features. You lean on words like "streamline" and "revolutionize." Your case studies read like press releases. And the PMs, supers, and VPs you are trying to reach scroll right past it. Not because your product is bad. Because your story doesn't sound like someone who has ever stood in a trailer.

What they wrote
"Streamline your project workflows with our AI-powered platform built for modern construction teams."
What a PM actually thinks
Cool. What does it do?

The gap isn't talent. Your marketing team is probably good. The gap is perspective. You need someone who has sat through the demos, managed the rollouts, and watched what actually gets adopted. Someone who can tell you what a VP of Preconstruction thinks when they read your homepage.

Two ways we work

Execution for founders. Judgment for teams.

Pre-Series A · No marketing hire

Fractional CMO for construction startups

You have a product that works and customer logos you are proud of. What you do not have is a marketing function. We run a three-stage engagement that diagnoses your positioning, rebuilds your core messaging, and builds the first content and distribution plan. Then we stay on, optionally, to keep the rhythm going.

This is execution, not advisory. We build it with you, then help you run it.

Post-Series A · Marketing team in place

Marketing advisor for construction tech leaders

Your team ships. Your channels produce leads. But the messaging feels corporate when it should feel field-grounded, and the VP at your ideal account still does not know who you are. We start with a front-loaded audit, then move to an advisory retainer: two strategy calls a month, asset reviews as your team ships, and a quarterly executive review.

This is advisory, not execution. We keep your team sharp. They do the work.
